Design language

How Stemma looks and speaks: like a careful report.

This summarizes how this site looks and speaks, from the design system in the repository, docs/stemma/02-design/DESIGN.md.

Identity

The mark is a small stemma codicum: one filled node for the earliest witness, and lines of descent to three open ones, one of them through an intermediate branch. It depicts no real manuscript tradition. Write the name Stemma, and stemma for the command. Keep clear space of a quarter of the mark's width around it, and do not use it below 16 pixels.

Voice

Tools and reports, not gates and approvals. Say what is wrong, show the evidence, say why it matters, suggest what to do.

These pages speak to an author, not a programmer: everyday words, you for the reader, and a term explained where it is first needed. What the tool prints is shown word for word, introduced in plain language and usually folded away until asked for.

Type

Three families under the SIL Open Font License, served from this site: Source Serif 4 for headings, Inter for text, JetBrains Mono for command output and identifiers. Source Serif 4 also sets quoted words, at its optical sizes and with its own italic. Their licence texts are published beside the font files: Source Serif 4, Inter, JetBrains Mono. Only Latin and extended Latin are bundled; other scripts fall back to system fonts.

Spacing and shape

Spacing steps of 4, 8, 12 and 16 pixels; 6-pixel control corners and 10-pixel card corners. Borders that carry meaning use the strong line. No shadows and no animation.

Components

Page layout

A page opens with its title. On wide screens each section's heading sits in the margin and stays in view while the section is read; on narrow screens it sits above the text. Running text keeps a short line; transcripts and entries may use the whole column.

Drawings

The research cycle, the demo's rounds of checking, where material lives and the example's page map are drawn with HTML and lines, so the words in each drawing are its text.

Line of descent

The drawing on the first page is a nested list of records with lines drawn between them, so its text is the drawing. As in the mark, the filled node is the one the rest descend from. Its caption says where it came from.

Status label

The script's own word in a bordered label. Never a check mark.

pass fail unverifiable

Report excerpt

Real command output in a monospace block. Its caption says what it is and what was replaced. Long lines wrap.

Finding

Fixed order: outcome and check, what is wrong, why it matters, what to do, evidence. The example is filled from a real JSON report.

unverifiable record.valid record_unreadable

A file cannot be read as a record, so nothing in it was checked.

Why it matters
Every other check reads these files. A broken record can hide a citation problem or create a false one.
What to do
Open the named file and repair it (look for merge-conflict markers, a repeated key or a missing bracket), or restore it from git. Stemma does not guess its content.

  • file: records/agents/agt-fixture0broken001.json
  • problem: duplicate_key

Figure

A screenshot or drawing has alternative text, a caption that says how it was made, and the same content available as text. A rendered page is never presented as a terminal window or as a Stemma screen.

Navigation and accessibility

The target is WCAG 2.2 level AA. A skip link comes first, each page has one first-level heading, the current page is marked with aria-current by weight and an underline, focus shows a 2-pixel ring, and wide tables and long excerpts can be reached by keyboard. On narrow screens the navigation wraps; the site needs no JavaScript.

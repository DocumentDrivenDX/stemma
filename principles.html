Principles

What Stemma does when two reasonable choices pull apart.

These are the rules Stemma is designed by, not a list of features. For what it does today, see How it works and Checks. The wording here is simplified. The project's exact wording is in its principles document.

  1. Tools and reports, not gates and approvals. Stemma points things out and explains them. It never stops you writing, or asks you to approve something before you can carry on.
  2. No source, no citation. A citation should rest on a source you hold. The assistant is told never to invent one, and a citation with no source to compare it with is shown as unchecked.
  3. Scripts verify, models propose. Checks are done by ordinary programs that give the same answer every time. An AI's opinion is never counted as a check.
  4. Verify what can be verified, and make the rest re-checkable. Whatever a program can check, it checks. The rest is set out so that you can check it easily and see who looked at what. No AI model writes or quietly corrects the text of a source.
  5. The author decides. Interpretation, structure, theory, method and what you disclose are your calls. The assistant helps; it does not settle them.
  6. Limits are stated. A clean result means only that the checks found nothing open. Notes and sign-offs are records of what someone said, not proof.
  7. Plain text and hashes. Your work is kept in ordinary files that you can read and compare, and that will outlast any one tool. Other files are recognized by a digital fingerprint.
  8. One source of truth. Everything Stemma tells you comes from the same set of records, so there is no second copy to fall out of step.
  9. Reproducible with the same inputs. Someone with your project, copies of the sources they are entitled to, and the same version of the tool should get the same check results.
  10. Respect what is not yours to share. Permissions and community limits matter. Stemma does not filter what reaches a hosted AI model, so material that may not be sent there does not belong in such a project.
  11. Deliverable over machinery. What helps authors comes before process for its own sake, without dropping checks that catch real mistakes.
  12. The plugin is the product. You install one plugin. It and Claude set up whatever else is needed, from versions that are fixed and verified, and you keep control of permissions.

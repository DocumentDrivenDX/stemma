Macclesfield Bank
:
this is a coral bank of great size, lying east of the Paracells ; some
parts of the bank are level, with a sandy bottom, but, generally, the
depth is very irregular. It is intersected by deep cuts or channels.
I am not able to perceive in the published charts, (its limits, how-
ever, are not very accurately known) whether the central part is
deeper, wdiich I suspect is the case, as in the great Chagos Bank, in
the Indian Ocean ; not coloured.

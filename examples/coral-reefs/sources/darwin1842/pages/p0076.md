FRINGING REEFS. 59
The three classes, atoll-formed, barrier, and fringing
reefs, together with the modifications just described of the
latter, include all the most remarkable coral-formations
anywhere existing. At the commencement of the last
chapter in the volume, where I detail the principles on
which the map (Plate III.) is coloured, the exceptional cases
will be enumerated.

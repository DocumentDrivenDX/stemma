Sandwich Arch. —Hawaii ; in the chart in Freycinet’s atlas,
small portions of the coast are fringed by reefs ; and in the accom-
panying Hydrog. Memoir, reefs are mentioned in several places, and
the coral is said to injure the cables. On one side of the islet of
Kohaihai there is a bank of sand and coral with five feet water on
it, running parallel to the shore, and leaving a channel of about fifteen
feet deep within. I have coloured this isld. red, but it is very much
less perfectly fringed than others of the group.

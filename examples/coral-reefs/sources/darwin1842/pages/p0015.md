Fig. 5.
—
New Caledonia, in the western part of the Pacific ; from
Krusenstern’s atlas, compiled from several surveys ; I have slightly
altered the northern point of the reef, in accordance with the atlas
of the voyage of the Astrolabe. Ill Krusenstern’s atlas, the reef is
represented by a single line with crosses; I have for the sake of
uniformity added an interior line ; scale ^ of an inch to a mile.

by Capt. Moresby and Lieut. Powell ; scale ^ of an inch to a mile.

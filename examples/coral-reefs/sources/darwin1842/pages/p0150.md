resembles either an upraised atoll with one half originally imper-
fect, or one unequally elevated ; and Anamouka, an atoll equally

bank : the highest part is only between twenty and thirty feet ; but
on this part, as well as on the rest of the land, (which as Cook
observes, rises above the height of true lagoon-islands,) coral-rock like
that on the beach, was found. In the Navigator Archipelago, Mr.
Couthouy§ found on Manila many and very large fragments of coral
at the height of eighty feet, “ on a steep hill-side, rising half a mile
inland from a low sandy plain abounding in marine remains." The
fragments were embedded in a mixture of decomposed lava and sand.
It is not stated whether they were accompanied by shells, or whether
the corals resembled recent species ; as these remains were embedded,
they possibly may belong to a remote epoch ; but I presume this
was not the opinion of Mr. Couthouy. Earthquakes are very
frequent in this archipelago.

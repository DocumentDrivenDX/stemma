With respect to fringing, or shore reefs, there is little in
their structure which needs explanation ; and their name
expresses their comparatively small extension. They dif-
fer from barrier- reefs in not lying so far from the shore, and
in not having within a broad channel of deep water. Reefs
also occur around submerged banks of sediment and of worn-
down rock ; and others are scattered quite irregularly w’here
the sea is very shallow : these in most respects are allied to
those of the fringing class, but they are of comparatively little
interest.

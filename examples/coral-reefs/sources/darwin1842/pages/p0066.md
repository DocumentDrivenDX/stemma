some encircled islands, is exceedingly improbable. And
why, again, should the reef spring up, in some cases steep on
both sides like a wall, at a distance of two, three, or more
miles from the shore, leaving a channel often between
200 and 300 feet deep, and rising, from a depth w^hich
we have reason to believe is destructive to the growth of
coral? An admission of this nature cannot possibly be
made. The existence, also, of the deep channel, utterly
precludes the idea of the reef having grown outwards, on a
foundation slowly formed on its outside, by the accumulation
of sediment and coral detritus. Nor, again, can it be as-

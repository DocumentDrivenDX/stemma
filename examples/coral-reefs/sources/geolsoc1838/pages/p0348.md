TRANSACTIONS
OF THE
GEOLOGICAL SOCIETY OF LONDON.
Volume IV., Second and concluding Part: 380 pages, 25 plates.
Price, to the Fellows, 17. 18s,; to the Public, 2/. 5s.
CONTENTS :
1V. Observations on some of the Strata between the Chalk and the Oxford
Oolite in the South-east of England; with extensive illustrations by
5 plates of coloured maps, views, and sections, and 14 plates of fossils.
By William Henry Fitton, M.D., P.G.S. F.R.S.
V. Zoologicai Observations on a New Fossil Species of Chelydra from
(Xningen ; with a lithographic drawing of the fossil. By Thomas
Bell, Esq., F.G.S., F.R.S.
VI. On the New Red Sandstone Series in the Basin of the Eden and North-
western Coasts of Cumberland and Lancashire; with a plate of co-
loured sections. By the Rev. Adam Sedgwick, V.P.G.S. F.R.S.,
Woodwardian Professor in the University of Cambridge.
VII. On a Portion of the Dukhun (Deccan), East Indies; with a map and
two plates of sections. By Lieut.-Col. William Henry Sykes, F.G.S.,
F.R.S., F.L.S.
VIII. On the Geology of the Environs of Bonn; with a coloured map. By
Leonard Horner, Esq., F.G.S., F.R.SS.L. & E.
Index, List of Donations, &c.

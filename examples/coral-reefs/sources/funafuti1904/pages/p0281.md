(1334) [722]. Length 140 millims. Greyish, mottled, mostly compact, with occasional cavities.

gastropods.
(134A) [723]. Length 85 millims. White, with a few greyish spots, porous, the white portions are

dolomite, whilst the interstices are in part empty, in part filled with white, opaque, detrital material.

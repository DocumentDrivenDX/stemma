Depth from Surface, 804-810 feet; Distance Bored 6 feet; Total Length of Core
Obtained, 5 feet 1 inch ;Numbers of Cores, 1574-174.
Cores cylindrical, of whitish, whitish-grey, partly mottled, dolomitic limestone;
hard (H = 4), partly compact, partly porous, in places cavernous, but not nearly to
the same extent as in the cores preceding. The cores on the whole are principally of
casts of corals, apparently in position of growth: they are badly preserved, and
usually enclosed by growths of Polytrema planum and Inthothamnion. The corals
include Pocillopora, Seriatopora, Celoria, Astrean not determined, Madrepora,
Turbinarit, Montipora, and Astreopora. The areas between the corals are filled
with fragmental materials; amongst these are foraminifera, aleyonarian spicules,
echinid spines, casts of Gastropods, Serpula, Halimeda, and in the finer sediment
the minute stellate spicules of Leptoclinum can be recognised. The organisms are
cemented by crystalline dolomite.

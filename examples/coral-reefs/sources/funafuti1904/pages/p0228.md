(12). Length 55 millims. &lt;A nodular rock with cavernous hollows, consisting of a clump of Pocilloporu.
The corallites are for the most part filled up solid; the coral has been extensively tunnelled by boring
organisms ; some of the borings remain empty, others are infilled by fine detrital mud, now consolidated
into hard rock. Polytrema miniaceum, Lithothamnion, with embedded orange-tinted tubes of Serpula.
(13) [506, 507, 608, 609]. Length 110 millims., diameter 100 millims. Cylindrical core nearly all of
Madrepora contectau, the corallites of the branches and some of the ccenenchyma filled in with fine

empty. Heliopora cerulea, fragment. Polytrema miniacewm. Lithothammon.
(14). About thirty irregular nodular fragments of hard, grey rock, worn by drill, the largest piece
50 millims., diameter 60 millims. Principally of corals: Helioporu cerulea, Pocillipora sp. with encrusting
Lathothamnion.
(15, 15bis.). Length 205 millims., diameter 103 millims. Core cylindrical, for the most part a
solid, compact, hard, mottled rock consisting of Heliopora cerulea, Porites (2), and Lithothamnion. Inter-
mediate areas are filled in with fine detrital sediment containing Polytrema nuniacewm, numerous echinid
spines, some of large size and of purple colour, and tubes of Serpula., The sediment is now, for the most
part, a hard rock, but in places it remains incoherent and powdery. Professor DAvID considers that this
core is part of a coral reef platform formed at low-water level.
(16) [610]. Nodular fragments (22) of hard cavernous rock, principally of Heliopora cerulea, Madrepora,
encrusting Polytrema planum, and Lithothainnion. P. miniacewm and Carpenteria also present. Largest
fragment 50 millims. long by 65 millims. wide.
(17, 18). Length 275 millims., diameter 103 millims. Cores cylindrical, largely of branching Litho-

partly as loose chalky powder. Orbitolites, Polytrema miniacewm, echinid spines, gastropod shells, small
coprolitic pellets, orange-tinted tubes of Serpula.

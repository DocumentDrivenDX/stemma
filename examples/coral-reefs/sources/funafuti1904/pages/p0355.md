GENUS ORBICELLA, DANA.
This genus is found rarely in the Main Boring between 82-914 feet ; below this to
the bottom of the boring it occurs frequently. It is also not uncommon in the First
and Second Borings. O. heliopora, Lam. and O. plesades, Eu. and Son, have
been determined ; other forms are allied to O. acropora, Lrxy., O. orion, DANA, and
O. funafutensis, GARD. J. S. Garpinert has described seven species now living on
the leeward reef and in the lagoon at Funafuti.

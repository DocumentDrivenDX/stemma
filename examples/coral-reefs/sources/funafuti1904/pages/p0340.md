Cylindrical cores, and irregular nodules of cream-coloured and whitish-grey, hard,
mostly compact limestone, of the same character as those from the higher parts of
the boring. The greater part of the rock consists of corals, Madrepora contecta
bemg still the predominant form; with it there are associated Pocillopora,
Orbicella(¢), Montipora, and detached aleyonarian spicules. Corals, generally filled
up solid with sclerenchyma, &c., and very obscurely shown. Foraminifera are seldom
met with im the cores, but in a sample of unconsolidated material from a depth of
70 feet they are abundant and varied. The following genera have been recognised
by Mr. Coapman: Cornuspira, Orbitolites, Sagrina, Globigerina, Pullenia,
Spirilina, Discorbina, Planorbulina, Pulvinulina, Calcarina, Tinoporus, Polytrema,
Amphistegina, and Heterostegina. Associated with these there are calcisponge
spicules, Cliona borings, echinid spines, Serpula, and Spirorbis, ostracoda, lamelli-
branchs, and encrusting Lithothammon.

(23). Length 38 millims. Of the same materials as the preceding core; a commingling of Heliopora
cerulea and Lithothamnion, with detrital mud containing echinid spines, Polytrema, coprolitic pellets, &e.
(24) [612, 613]. Length 120 millims. Core cylindrical, consisting of a very cavernous mass of Heliopora
cerulea, an undetermined coral, Polytrema planum and Lithothamnion. Stellates of Leptoclinum, abundant in
mud, echinid spines, Carpenteria and P. miniaceum. Professor DAvin states that this is an undoubted reef
rock in situ.
(25). Length 69 millims. Like the preceding. The Heliopora is growing in thick upright lamin, with
the structure preserved; it is extensively tunnelled by Cliona sponges and other organisms. In the detrital
calcareous mud there are large purple echinid spines and a few joints of Halimeda.
(26) [614]. Length 50 millims. Nodular lump, mainly of Lithothannion, with some small pieces of
Heliopora cerulea; encrusting Polytrema planum abundant, frequently alternating with Lithothamnion.
Echinid spines, Leptoclinum spicules, Orbitolites, Globigerina, rare.
(27-31) [509, 615]. Rounded nodular pieces of core, with an aggregate length of 171 millims. Consist-
ing of Heliopora and Lithothamnion, generally similar to the preceding. Halimeda, stellate spicules of
Leptoclinum, minute fragments of Heliopora, &c., in the consolidated mud. Placopsilina.
(32) [508, 627]. Length 118 millims. Cylindrical core, consisting principally of a perforate coral, so
infilled with sclerenchyma that it cannot be determined with certainty. It may possibly belong to
Madrepora. The coral is overgrown by successive thin layers of Lithothamnion. Partially coherent
foraminiferal and fragmental material, containing Orbitolites, Polytrema miniaceum, Leptoclinum stellates, and
coprolitic pellets, surround the coral.
(33). Length 95 millims. Hard greyish, very cavernous core, consisting of Heliopora cerulea, an
undetermined perforate coral, branching Lithothamnion, and echinid spines.

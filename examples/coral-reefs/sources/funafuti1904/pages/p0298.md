(338A, 3394). Total length 192 millims. Whitish-grey and grey, hard, mostly compact rock, with

(340A). Length 215 millims, Greyish-white, hard, compact to porous. About two-thirds of this core

consists of fragmental detritus with Polytrema, Amphistegina and Lithothamnion, cemented by dolomite.

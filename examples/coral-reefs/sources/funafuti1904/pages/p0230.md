DETAILS.
(34). Length 90 millims. Cavernous, mostly of compressed or cylindrical stems of Heliopora cerulea,
laminate Montipora, and branching Lithothamnion. 'The Heliopora is now of a dirty greenish tint, the
structure is well preserved, and the tubes of the interior are not infilled. Echinid spines, Polytrena
miniaceum, retaining the pinkish tint, P. planwm, Orhitolites.
(35). Length 55 millims. Hard, compact rock, principally of Heliopora cerulea intergrown with Millepora
and Montipora, Lithothamnion, Serpula.
(36) [616]. Length 47 millims. Generally similar to preceding, but only Millepora and Montipora are
present, the corals are overgrown by Lithothamnion and some Polytrema planum. The hardened sediment
also contains Carpenteria, P. miniacewm, Leptoclinum stellates, branching fragments of Lithothamnion, and
echinid spines.
(37). Length 73 millims. Very cavernous, hard rock, consisting of clumps of Pocillopora and Montipora.
The Pocillopora is tunnelled by Cliona, &c., the tunnels and the corallites are infilled with white sediment.
Lithothamnion, Halimeda-joints, Polytrena miniaceum, Serpula and echinid spines.
(38). Length 76 millims., diameter 103 millims. Cavernous, consisting of Millepora, Heliopora cerulea,
Montipora (2), branching Lithothamnion, Halimeda-joints, large purplish echinid spines, Serpula, Polytrema
miniaceum.
(39-47) [617]. Nine irregular lumps of hard, partly cavernous rock, with a total length of 243 millims.
The rock chiefly of the following corals, Millepora, Heliopora ceerulea, Stylophora and Montipora (2). The
structure of the Stylophora well preserved ; the coral interspaces partly empty, partly filled with sediment
or with prisms of “conchite.” It isin places encrusted by Lithothamnion. 'The hardened sediment contains
Orlitolites, Carpenteria, Polytrema nuniaceum ; stellate spicules of Leptoclinum, and large purple-tinted echinid
spines.
(48). Length 50 milims. Cavernous, hard rock, composed of Millepora, encrusted by Lithothamnion,
Heliopora cerulea and Stylophora. Numerous large purplish echinid spines, sub-triangular in transverse
section.
(49-51) [510, 618]. Length 56 millims. Irregular nodules, of hard compact rock worn by drill, consist-
ing of Heliopora caerulea, Stylophora and Montipora (2). The corals overgrown by finely laminate Lithothamnion
and Polytrema planum. In the consolidated mud, Orbitolites, Placopsilina, Planorbulina, Carpenteria,
P. miniaceum.
(52) [619, 6194]. Length 158 millims., diameter 103 millims. Cylindrical core, consisting of a mass of
Madrepora contecta, showing in a vertical section the branches completely enveloped by the coenenchyma,
The branches, 16-23 millims., in width, are formed of closely arranged, fairly thick-walled, radial corallites,
for the most part solidly infilled with sclerenchyma and fibrous carbonate of lime. The ccenenchymal
interstices in the central portions of the coral are empty, but near the margins they are infilled with
sediment. The coral appears to be in its position of growth.
(53). Length 54 millims. An irregular lump, principally of Madrepora contecta, encrusted by
Lathothannion.
(54). Length 28 millims. A cavernous piece of core with fragments of Heliopora cerulea, Pocillopora,
and perforate coral, overgrown by Lithothumnion, Polytrema miniaceum, Serpula.
(55). Length 147 millims. A massive cylindrical core, cavernous in places, chiefly composed of thick
stems and laminate walls of Heliopora cwrulea and also of Millepora. Surrounding the corals are partially
consolidated masses of fragmental materials, with Polytrema, echinid spines, &c. A nearly perfect sea-
urchin, determined by Professor JEFFREY BELL to be Cidaris metularia, Lam., occurs in the detritus.

small admixture of Orbitolites and Polytrema. Inthothamnion is present, encrusting
corals, and so are detached joints of Halimeda; also echinid spines. and casts of
gastropods. The samples of powdered rock from 448 feet are generally fine, they
contain pieces of Pocilopora and perforate corals, with Amplhestegina.

(214). From 430 to 433 feet. Length 40 millims. An irregular lump of hard whitish rock, porous and
cavernous, mainly composed of casts of Seriatopora with Amphistegina and empty casts of gastropods,
Echinid spines.
(215). Length 110 millims. by 55 millims. in diameter. Cylindrical core of whitish grey rock, porous.
Mainly foraminiferal with fine sediment, cemented by calcite. No corals recognised. A microscopic
section [655] shows that the predominant form is Calcarina hispida, with some Orbitolites, and Polytrema
planum. Halimeda joints, common ; Lithothamnion in small fragments.
(216). Length 88 millims. Cylindrical core of hard whitish-grey rock, minutely porous, consisting of
foraminifera and Halimeda, like preceding ; also cast of a piece of Seriatopora.
(217). Length 85 millims. Core cylindrical, rock cavernous with branching Seriatopora and Madrepora,
the coral casts are encrusted by Lithothamnion. Calcarina, and Orbitolites.
(218, 219). Two small cylindrical cores with a total length of 50 millims. Whitish-grey rock, like
preceding, of casts of Seriatopora and encrusting Lithothamnion. The parasitic borings in the corals now
appear as irregular anastomosing calcareous networks in the cavities from which the coral structures have
been dissolved out.
(220, 221, 222). Three rubbly fragments of rock similar to the preceding with casts of Seriatopora and

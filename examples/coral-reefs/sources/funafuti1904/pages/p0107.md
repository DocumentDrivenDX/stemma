The deposit O.8 may be defined as a zone of dead Lithothamnion and forami-
niferal rock, more or less of the nature of a breccia, with a smooth or slightly
mammullated surface. It lies next inshore to the living Lithothamuion zone of the
ocean platform, and is carpeted in places with small green algze, replaced occasionally,
as at Funamanu, by ochreous algee. In addition to Lithothamnion, Polytrema planwn
has played an important part in building this stratum. A reference to Plate 17,
Section on line C-D, by Mr. G. H. Hatiican, shows the general relation of this crust
of dead Lithothannion, Polytrema, Carpenteria, &e., to the old Heliopora caerulea
reef, O.L.1. This section may be taken as a type of what obtains elsewhere on the
eastern rim of the atoll.
It will be noticed that towards the ocean edge of the O.3 zone the deposit rise
first, then falls over so as to form a low rim about 6 to 7 inches above the general level
of the small flat inside it. This flat, bounded seawards by the rim, is occupied
by a series of long shallow pools at low tide. Accurate levels, taken with a 12-inch
dumpy level, by Mr. Hattrean, show that the top of this rim is 2°82 feet above
low water, whereas the bottom of the flat inside it is only about 2°22 feet above
the same datum. &lt;A glance at the deposit which comes next outside, O.L.10, the
living Lithothamnion zone, shows a repetition of the shallow flat and raised rim, the
levels in this case being respectively 1°21 and 2°22 feet above low water.

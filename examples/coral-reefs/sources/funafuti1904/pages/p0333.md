Sample of loose materials, from a depth of 90 feet, consisting of small subangular particles of broken
up whitish limestone, with numerous foraminifera and other organisms. The genera most frequently
represented are Orbitolites, Amphistegina and Heterostegina ;other genera of less common occurrence are
Miliolina, Textularia, Verneuilina, Truncatulina, Carpenteria, Pulvinulina, Calearina and Gypsina. Broken
fragments of perforate corals are also present, together with alcyonarian spicules, echinid spines, polyzoa,
small molluscan shells, fragments of Halimeda, and numerous small coprolitic ovoid pellets. Microscopic
sections of some of these pellets show that they are composed of minute fragments of foraminifera, corals,

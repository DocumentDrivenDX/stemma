b. The Crystallisation of the Fine Calcareous Detritus.
Deposition from solution is not the only process by which secondary calcite and
aragonite are formed. Another change is observed as the cores are studied in
descending order which also results in the formation of these minerals. This is not,
however, one which affects the empty spaces of the rock, but those which contain
finely divided calcareous detritus.

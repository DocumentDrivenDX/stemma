The solid cores consist of several irregular fragments and three cylindrical cores of
a greyish-white, moderately hard and very porous limestone, in which there are
a few obscure casts of corals, apparently of Madrepora, and a compound fungid coral.
The greater portion of the cores seems to consist of foraminifera, with aleyonarian
spicules and casts of small gastropods. The incoherent materials, brought to the
surface by the sand-pump, are fine and moderately coarse chips of a rock similar to
that of the solid cores; the only organisms seen with a lens are foraminifera, broken
echinid spines, and very rarely a small piece of a cast of coral. Professor Davin
considers that these represent cuttings through fine coral rubble.

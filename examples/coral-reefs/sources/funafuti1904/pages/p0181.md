(D.) Observations on the temperature of the sea water surrounding an atoll.
The following few observations were taken at Onoatoa, in the Gilbert group, by means
of a minimum thermometer kindly lent me by Mr. H. C. Russexi, F.R.S., during
dredging operations about 14 miles off the shore, on November 25th, 1898.
Temperature of air at the time (10 o'clock a.m.), 86° Fahrenheit.
Surface water: 81° F. At 60 fathoms, 80° F.
At 17 fathoms, 81° F. At 90 fathoms, 723° F.
* The glass flask in which this gas was collected was unfortunately broken during transit.

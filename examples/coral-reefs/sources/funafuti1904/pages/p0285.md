254 DR. G. J. HINDE.

<!-- stemma:block role="running-head" basis="repeated-line" -->
Depth from Surface, 810-815 feet; Distance Bored, 5 feet; Total length of Core

<!-- stemma:block role="undetermined" -->
Obtained, 3 feet 6 inches ;Numbers of Cores, 1754-1864.
Cores cylindrical, greyish-white, often banded or speckled, hard, partly compact
and dense, partly porous and cavernous dolomite. Corals are generally present, but
now frequently replaced and infilled solid with crystalline dolomite, and their characters
mostly obliterated. This applies more particularly to the perforate forms ; in the
Astrzean corals the structures are often dissolved and not infilled with dolomite, but
even under these conditions they can only approximately be determined. They
are included in Astrea, Goniastraa, Cycloseris, Madrepora, and Porites. The corals
are, as usual, encrusted by layers of Polytrema and Inthothamnion.
A considerable amount of fragmental detritus with foraminifera is present in the
cores. The most abundantly represented are Orbitolites, Curpenteria, Amphistegina,
and Heterostegina. Aleyonarian spicules, echinid spines, and in the finer sediment,
stellates of Leptoclinum are present. Halineda-joints.

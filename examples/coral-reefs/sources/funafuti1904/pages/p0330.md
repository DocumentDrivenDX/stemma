Depth from Surface, 30-50 feet; Distance Bored, 20 feet; Total Length of Core
Obtained, 2 feet 3 inches; Numbers of Cores, C. 6-C. 15.
The cores are partly cylindrical and very cavernous, partly irregular fragments
of cream-coloured, greyish-white and greenish limestone. They principally consist of
corals, masses of Heliopora caerulea being the commonest form, Orbicella, Psam-
mocora, and Montipora. The corals are in part embedded in a fine, hard sediment
containing Nubecularia, Miliolina, Orbitolites, Valuulina, Planorbulina, Carpenteria,
Gypsina inherens, Polytrema planum, and Amphistegina. Also echinid spines,
Spirorbis, Leptoclinum stellates, detached calcisponge spicules; infilled Cliona
borings and Lithothammion.

We anchored in twelve fathoms, about three quarters of a mile off Wotje |
Island. Corals grow in large patches between our anchorage and the shore,
mainly blocks of Porites, patches of Pocillipores, of Millepores, and of Mad-
repores; wide patches of Algz and Nullipores, separated by lanes of sand,
extend to seven or eight fathoms in depth. Along the slope of the beaches
both the corals and the Algz diminish in number with increasing depth,
to twelve or fifteen fathoms, and more rapidly to twenty fathoms. The.
island of Wotje (Pl. 227, fig. 4) is fully three quarters of a mile wide;it

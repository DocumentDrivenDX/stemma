FIGURE 275.—Core (K-l-1-13) from the 100- to 103-foot
interval in hole K-l on Engebi. Coral stained with
Meigen's solution to differentiate the aragonite (dark
gray) from the calcite (light). The original aragonite
corallum is being replaced by coarse calcite crystals
along a jagged front and in discrete areas. Addition of
calcite has resulted in the filling of interseptal spaces.
The boundary between the original skeletal aragonite
and the secondary calcite is marked by a thin saw-
toothed film of chalky aragonite. X 2.

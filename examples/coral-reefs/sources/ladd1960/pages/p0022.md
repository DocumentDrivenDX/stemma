Interval (feet) Lithologic character
64. 5-69. 5 Coral, irregular cores massive; as long as 5 in;
coral surfaces show encrustations of algae and
Foraminifera; well-lithified coarse debris of
algae and Foraminifera in a chalky silty sand
matrix packed between the coral; parts of the
coral show irregular and patchy recrystalliza-
tion to medium-grained calcite crystals; surface
of the core slightly vuggy; below the first two
pieces of coral core (total about 12 in) the re-
mainder of the run is limestone composed of
packed Halimeda segments in a chalky sandy
matrix; segments generally unaltered but a
few show minor effects of solution.
69. 5-80 Coral similar to last; shows recrystalizatioii to
calcite; numerous clusters of light-tan to white
calcite crystals; Halimeda segments and some
coral fragments show partial alteration to cal-
cite but much debris, including echinoid spires,
Heliopora, and worn fragments of mollusk
shells, unaltered; sand fraction contains nu-
merous worn tests of Marginopora, Amphisle-
gina, and Calcarina.
80-85 Limestone, algal; upper 4 inches of core of a lacey
framework with intersecting voids partially
packed by foraminiferal sand; lower part of
cored section made up of unaltered, massive
coral totaling about 10 in; no recrystallization
or calcite deposition.
85-90 No sample.

diameter; alternate with Halimeda-ricli lime-
stone; coral shows some void filling by fine
material but no recrystallization; Halimeda
segments set in a sandy foraminiferal matrix,
show no alteration but are well cemented.
97. 5-100 No sample.

HARD ROCK BEIOW BEACH ROCK ZONE
Most of the 17 holes revealed some hard rock below
intertidal levels, which were mostly conglomerate and
massive coral heads. Many of the conglomerate
deposits were well cemented but the massive coral
heads in many places were completely unaltered. The
most unusual of the holes that showed hard rock was
RU-2, where, from a depth of 30 feet to the bottom of
the hole at 135 feet, the entire section seemed to be
hard, most of it recrystallized and firmly cemented by
calcite. The possible significance of this hole is discussed
under the section on structure.

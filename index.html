Research and write with Claude. The Stemma plugin keeps your sources in order, checks your citations against your own copies of them, and explains what it finds in plain language.

Why rigor takes more

An AI assistant can produce a reference that does not exist, give a real title the wrong author, cite a page the work does not have, or invent a quotation.

Checking all of that by hand can cost the time the assistant saved. With Stemma, the comparing that a computer can do reliably is done for you, against your own copies. The report also tells you what it did not check, so you know where to look yourself.

Research in rounds

Research is rarely finished in one pass. You find another source, get a better scan, or rewrite a sentence. Stemma fits that loop.

How a round works, step by step

Every citation has a family tree

Scholars draw a stemma codicum to show which copy of a manuscript comes from which. Stemma does something similar for your work: each citation leads back to a record of the source, and from there to the copy you hold.

What gets checked

Your sources
Do you have a copy of each work you cite, and is its text still the same? If you replace a copy, you see what relied on the old one.
Your quotations
Are the words in your copy's text? If something close is there instead, you see both side by side.
Your page numbers
Is each page you cite actually in the work, or among the pages numbered in your copy?
Your references
Does each citation point to one source? Is each work you list actually cited? Are the project files in order? You are also warned about map coordinates written into them.

What stays yours

Judgment stays with you. A pass only means that your record agrees with the text taken from your copy, and that text can be wrong, especially when a computer read it from a scan. No check tells you that a source supports your argument.

Stemma does not check authors, titles or dates against a library catalogue or your copies, or whether a figure you quote appears in the source. It does not read your manuscript files; it checks the citations that have been recorded. Nothing ever stops you from writing.

Stemma

A Claude plugin that helps scholars write books with checked citations. Ask Claude in plain words; scripts check your project against your own stored copies and explain what they find.

Pre-release.

Install and ask Claude See real output

The name

A stemma codicum is the family tree of a text's manuscripts: it shows which copy derives from which. Stemma gives a book's citations a line of descent to a stored source.

What it does

  • Keeps sources and citations as plain record files in your project.
  • Keeps copies of your sources and their text in a private store outside the project, with where and when each came from.
  • Checks that each cited source is held with text that still matches, that citation keys resolve, that listed bibliography entries are cited, and that record files are well formed. It warns about coordinates written in project files.
  • Explains every finding: what is wrong, the evidence, why it matters and what to do. Nothing is blocked.

Part of a family

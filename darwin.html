Worked example: Darwin and coral reefs

One real source and three citations of it, kept as record files in the repository.

The copy of the book and its text are private and are not published. Only records and digests are. A model read the facts below from images of the stored pages; no person has confirmed them.

The source

The Structure and Distribution of Coral Reefs. Being the First Part of the Geology of the Voyage of the Beagle, under the Command of Capt. Fitzroy, R.N. during the Years 1832 to 1836. Charles Darwin. London: Smith, Elder and Co., 1842.

The title and year were read from the image of the title page. The text layer of the same page, from unverified character recognition, gives one of the voyage's years wrongly and does not hold the year of publication.

What cites it

These sentences are paraphrases written for the example, each compared with the image of the cited page. They quote nothing.

PageSentence
88Darwin's fifth chapter sets out a theory of how the different classes of coral reefs formed.
89Darwin calls Chamisso's account of atolls an earlier and better theory than the crater theory, and argues that it still does not apply to most atolls.
2Darwin says that he uses the term atoll throughout the volume as a synonym for lagoon-island.

What a check says without the private copy

Where the stored copy is not present, Stemma cannot check these citations, and says so. With it, all three citations passed source.held on the maintainer's machine on 2026-11-26; that run depends on the private store and is not reproduced here. Even then the project is not called checked clean: its report lists checks of cited sources that were not run.

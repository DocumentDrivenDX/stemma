Worked example: Darwin and coral reefs

A planned end-to-end example on a real historical subject.

In progress. Nothing of this example has been built or checked yet. It has no Stemma project, records, claims, quotations, maps or typeset pages. This page makes no statement about what any of these works argue.

Where it stands

Copies of candidate sources are kept on the maintainer's machine, outside the repository. The works below are those whose title or opening pages have been read so far, in the text layer of the stored copy. That text comes from character recognition and has not been compared with page images. These are not Stemma source records.

TitleName givenWhat the stored text shows
The Structure and Distribution of Coral ReefsCharles DarwinLondon: Smith, Elder and Co. The preface is dated May 1842.
The Structure and Distribution of Coral Reefs, third editionCharles Darwin, with an appendix by T. G. BonneyLondon: Smith, Elder, & Co., 1889.
The Life and Letters of Charles Darwin, volume IIIEdited by Francis DarwinLondon: John Murray, 1887.
The Glacial-Control Theory of Coral ReefsReginald A. DalyProceedings of the American Academy of Arts and Sciences, volume 51, number 4, November 1915.

What has to happen first

  • Stemma has to store a copy of a source and extract its text. Neither is built.
  • Quotation and locator checks have to be built before this page quotes anything.
  • Publication rights have to be confirmed for each copy before it enters the repository.

Until then, the invented examples show what works.
